# Dear Data 100: How dogs and humans read each other

Topic: how dogs read human signals (tone, facial expression, gesture, emotional vibe) and how humans read dog signals (body language, movement, vocalisation, emotional cues).

Data: a mix of
- observations of my own dog (anonymous, no personal details), and
- findings from published research, each recorded with its source.

Each record: date or source, signal, who sent it, the response, and the source (my own observation, or the study or article it came from).

Goal: at least 100 observations, visualised in Processing.
