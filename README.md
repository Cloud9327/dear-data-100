Dear Data 100: How dogs and humans read each other
Topic
How dogs read human signals (tone, facial expression, gesture, emotional vibe) and how humans read dog signals (body language, movement, vocalisation, emotional cues).

Data
A mix of:

observations of my own dog (anonymous, no personal details)
findings from published research, each recorded with its source
Current dataset size: 31 observations.

Data Structure
Each row contains:

Date
Source
Sender
Channel
Signals
Response
Intensity
Source_Note
Column Descriptions
Date: date of the observation or publication
Source: own observation or published study
Sender: human or dog
Channel: facial expression, gesture, tone of voice, body language, vocalisation, movement, emotional vibe, etc.
Signals: the specific behaviour or cue observed
Response: reaction to that signal
Intensity: rating from 1–5
Source_Note: observation notes or a reference link to the original source

Example Record

Date: 2026-10-15
Source: Own Observation
Sender: Human
Channel: Tone of Voice
Signals: Firm voice for a command
Response: She obeys and sits down
Intensity: 4
Source_Note: Giving her a treat; asked her to sit first

Goal
Collect at least 100 observations and create a Processing visualisation showing patterns in how dogs and humans interpret each other's signals.
